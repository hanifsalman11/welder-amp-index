Frequently Asked Questions (16 Gauge Steel)

What thickness of steel can the 16 Gauge Steel weld?

With 200 Amps of maximum output, the 16 Gauge Steel can weld from 24-gauge sheet metal (at 30–40 Amps with 0.023" wire) up to 3/8-Inch (single/multi-pass) & 1/4-Inch (single-pass) using 0.030" or 0.035" wire and proper joint beveling.

What wire speed (IPM) and voltage should I use for 1/8-inch steel on the 16 Gauge Steel?

For 1/8-inch (0.125" / 11-gauge) mild steel using 0.030" ER70S-6 solid wire and 75/25 Argon/CO2 gas, set voltage to 18.5V – 19.5V and wire feed speed to 212 – 230 Inches Per Minute (IPM) (~125 Amps).

What size breaker and outlet do I need for the 16 Gauge Steel?

On 240V operation, install a NEMA 6-50R receptacle backed by a 30-Amp 2-pole circuit breaker. If running on a 120V outlet, use a dedicated 20-Amp breaker with a 12 AWG cord so the breaker does not trip at 90–125A output.
